About Us

We are an independent energy consultant entity that helps SME and LARGE/ENTERPRISE business customers across the UK to find the best prices from the live energy market. Our offering has developed over 20 years to ensure SME and LARGE/ENTERPRISE customers save on their monthly and yearly energy bills on acquisition, renewal and contract upgrades.

Complaints Procedure

We at Axen Business House (UK) Limited pride ourselves on excellent customer service and the manner in which we conduct our business. However, we also understand that things can go wrong. We take all complaints very seriously and are committed to continuously improving our services. If you are unhappy with any of our sales activities, marketing activities, or the general level of service you have received, we will do our utmost to put things right.

If you wish to make a complaint, you can do so in several ways:

  • Write to us at: 16 Centreway, Ilford, IG1 1ND
  • Telephone us on: +44 203 372 4735
  • Email us at: complaints@axenuk.com

Any client who makes a complaint will be treated with the utmost courtesy and respect. We will investigate your complaint and keep you informed of the expected timescales for resolution. We strive to resolve all complaints within 48–72 working hours. In some instances this may take longer; if so, we will notify you in writing if more than 48–72 working hours pass without resolution.

If after 7 days your complaint remains unresolved, or if you are unhappy with the outcome, we will — with your consent — escalate your complaint to the Director of Axen Business House (UK) Limited. If you remain dissatisfied with the Director's decision, or if your complaint has been unresolved for more than 8 weeks, you have the right to refer your complaint to the Energy Ombudsman. This service is impartial and free to use.

Energy Ombudsman contact details:

  • Email: enquiry@energyombudsman.org
  • Website: www.energyombudsman.org
