About Us

We are an independent energy consultant entity that helps SME and LARGE/ENTERPRISE business customers across the UK to find the best prices from the live energy market. Our offering has developed over 20 years to ensure SME and LARGE/ENTERPRISE customers save on their monthly and yearly energy bills on acquisition, renewal and contract upgrades.

While offering the best prices, we provide professional advice to our valued customers regarding any dispute between our customers and energy suppliers, meter issues, power cuts, new connections and new metering. At Axen Business House, we help businesses throughout the UK to take control of their utilities in order to save money, save time and cut carbon in the quest to achieve net zero.

Imagine saving up to 45% on your annual electricity, gas and water bills – that represents a substantial amount of savings for any business. That 45% annual saving may be worth hundreds of thousands of GBP which most customers do not realise. Our highly experienced and professional team is ready to assist any business customer within the UK to achieve such savings. This effectively means our customers can add those savings to their annual revenue and run their business with peace of mind.

Complaints Procedure

We at Axen Business House (UK) Limited pride ourselves on excellent customer service and the manner in which we conduct our business. However, we also understand that things can go wrong, and we take all complaints very seriously and are committed to continuously improving our services. If you are unhappy with any of our sales activities, marketing activities or just the general level of service you have received, we will do our utmost to correct these issues.

If you are unhappy and wish to make a complaint you can do so in several ways:

  • Write to us at: Flat 803, 450, Charter House, High Road, Ilford, Essex, IG1 1ZH
  • Telephone us on: 07405439900
  • Email us at: admin@axenuk.com

Any client who makes a complaint will be treated with the utmost courtesy and respect. We will investigate and inform you of the likely timescales to resolve the issues. We strive to deal with any complaint within 48-72 working hours, but in some instances, this can take longer; if this is the case we will inform you of this in writing should more than 48-72 working hours lapse without resolution.

If after 7 days your complaint remains unresolved or if you are unhappy with the resolution, with your consent we will escalate your complaint to the Director of AXEN BUSINESS HOUSE (UK) LIMITED. If you are still unhappy with the decision of the Director; or we have not been able to resolve your complaint; or if it has been unresolved for more than 8 weeks, you have the right to escalate your complaint to Ombudsman Services. This service is impartial and free for clients to use.

www.ombudsman-services.org/energy.html
